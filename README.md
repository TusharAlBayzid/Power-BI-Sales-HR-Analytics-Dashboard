# 📊 Power BI Analytics Portfolio: Sales & HR Dashboards

Welcome to my Power BI data visualization portfolio! This repository showcases a comprehensive, two-part analytics project covering both commercial sales performance and organizational human resources (HR) metrics. 

## 🚀 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to transform raw, disconnected datasets into interactive, actionable dashboards. The project is divided into two major challenges, highlighting proficiency in data extraction, Star Schema modeling, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions), and professional UI/UX visualization principles.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI
* **Data Transformation:** Power Query
* **Data Modeling:** Star Schema (1-to-Many Relationships)
* **Calculations:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

---

## 📈 Challenge 1: Sales Performance Dashboard
A dynamic dashboard designed to track commercial success, monitor brand performance, and analyze regional sales trends.

**Key Features & Visualizations:**
* **Interactive Slicers:** Filtering by Division, District, and Beverage Brand.
* **KPI Cards:** Quick insights into `Total Revenue`, `Total Units Sold`, and `Average Price`.
* **Trend Analysis (Line Charts):** Month-wise and Day-wise sales trajectory.
* **Brand Contribution (Donut Chart):** Total revenue distribution across various beverage brands.
* **Profitability Analysis (Clustered Bar Chart):** A comparative view of Total Profit vs. Total Revenue by brand.

---

## 👥 Challenge 2: HR Analytics Dashboard
An in-depth organizational dashboard built to monitor workforce demographics, employee retention, and overall job satisfaction.

**Key Features & Visualizations:**
* **Core KPIs (Cards):** Tracking `Total Employees (Since Inception)`, `Current Employees`, `Terminated Employees`, `Total Salary Paid`, and `Employee Termination Rate`.
* **Workforce Demographics:**
  * Gender distribution among current employees (Donut Chart).
  * Employee education levels (Bar Chart).
* **Employee Well-being:**
  * Job satisfaction ratings (Column Chart).
  * Work-life balance distribution (Pie Chart).
* **Hiring & Retention Trends:**
  * Quarterly New Hires vs. Quarterly Terminations (Column Charts).
  * Monthly Termination and Salary Trends (Line Charts).
* **Salary Spread Analysis:** 
  * Analyzing salary distribution by years at the company and total working years, categorized by Employment Type and Gender (Area/Line/Scatter Charts).

---

## 💡 Key Learnings & Skills Demonstrated
* **Advanced DAX:** Created dynamic measures using `CALCULATE`, `SUMX`, `DIVIDE`, and logical filters.
* **Data Cleansing:** Handled data formatting and hierarchy adjustments (e.g., extracting specific Quarter/Month trends from raw dates).
* **Dashboard Design:** Applied clean aesthetics, avoiding clutter, and selecting the most appropriate charts for data distribution and spread.
